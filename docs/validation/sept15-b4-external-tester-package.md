# Vendor Assurance Portal — External Tester Guide

Thank you for helping us test the SecureLogic AI Vendor Assurance Portal.

You are playing the role of a **vendor's compliance contact** who has been
asked by a customer to complete a short security questionnaire and attach a
document. We want to know whether a first-time user can get from the invitation
email to a confirmation screen without help, and where they get confused.

This is a **test environment**. Nothing you enter is real, nothing is sent to a
real customer, and you cannot break anything. Please use **made-up answers** and
a **sample document** (any PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PNG, JPEG, text or CSV
file you are happy for us to see — for example a page of placeholder text).
Do not upload anything confidential or personal.

We are testing the product, not you. If something is unclear, that is a result.

---

## Before you start

- Use whatever device and browser you would normally use for work. Note which
  ones (for example "Windows laptop, Chrome" or "iPhone, Safari").
- Have a sample document ready to attach (under 25 MB).
- Have a way to take notes or screenshots as you go.
- Start a timer when you open the email; note the time at each step.

Your invitation link works for 30 days and you can come back to it — if you
close the tab, opening the link again brings you back to where you were.

---

## The task

Please do the steps below **in order** and note anything that surprises you.

### Step 1 — Receive the invitation

You will get an email from us containing a link that begins with
`https://securelogic-app-staging.onrender.com/portal/accept/…`

- Did the email make it clear who is asking and what they want?
- Was it obvious what to click?

### Step 2 — Open the portal

Click the link. You should land on an **Overview** page showing the name of the
organisation requesting the assessment, the request's status, and how much is
left to do.

- Did the page load without errors?
- Did you understand where you were and what you were expected to do next?
- Note anything on the page you did not understand.

### Step 3 — Complete the questionnaire

Go to the **Questionnaire** tab. For each question, choose an answer
(**Pass**, **Partial**, **Fail** or **Not applicable**) and, if you like, add a
short note. Answers save as you go — look for a "saved" indication.

- Answer every question. Use any answers you like; they are not being judged.
- Try changing one answer after you have saved it.
- Note any question whose wording you found unclear, any "why are we asking"
  explanation that did not help, and whether it was obvious that your work was
  being saved.

### Step 4 — Attach a document

Go to the **Attachments** tab. Attach your sample document. You can give it a
description (for example "Sample security policy") and, if you want, link it to
one of the questions.

- Did the upload succeed? Was it clear that it had?
- Try removing the document and attaching it again.
- Note what happened if anything was rejected, and whether the message told
  you why.

### Step 5 — (Optional) Send a message

Go to the **Messages** tab and send a short message such as "Test message from
the external tester". This is where you would ask the requesting organisation
a question.

### Step 6 — Review and submit

Go to **Review & submit**. Check the summary, then press **Submit responses**.

- Before pressing submit, did the page tell you clearly what submitting means
  (that you cannot change answers or attachments afterwards)?
- After submitting, you should see a confirmation page ("Responses submitted").
- Go back to the Questionnaire and Attachments tabs. Is it clear that they are
  now locked?

### Step 7 — Sign out

Use the sign-out control. Then open your original invitation link again and
note what happens.

**Stop the timer.**

---

## What we would like you to note

For each step:

1. **Time taken** (roughly).
2. **Confusions** — anything you had to read twice, guess at, or that used
   words you did not understand.
3. **Dead ends** — any point where you did not know what to do next, or a
   button/link that did nothing or went somewhere unexpected.
4. **Errors** — any error message (please quote it or screenshot it) and what
   you were doing at the time.
5. **Visual problems** — text cut off, things overlapping, hard to read on your
   screen size.
6. **What worked well** — this matters too.

At the end:

- Device and browser (and screen size if unusual).
- Overall: on a scale of 1 (very hard) to 5 (very easy), how easy was it to get
  from the email to the confirmation page?
- Would you have needed to contact someone for help at any point? Where?
- Anything you expected to be able to do and could not.

---

## How to report back

Reply to the invitation email with your notes — a plain list is perfect.
Screenshots are very welcome. If you prefer, we can do a 20-minute call where
you walk us through what you found.

If you get completely stuck, do not spend more than 10 minutes on it — write
down where you got stuck and email us. That result is as useful as a completed
run.

Please send your notes within **5 working days** of receiving the invitation
if you can.

---

## Consent and scope

- This is a usability test of a pre-release product on a test system. Your
  participation is voluntary and you can stop at any time.
- We will record what you do on the test system (pages visited, answers and
  files you submit, the messages you send) so that we can see where the
  experience failed. We will not share your name outside the SecureLogic AI
  team without asking you first.
- Please use only made-up answers and a non-confidential sample document.
- Please do not try to bypass the product's intended flow, access anything
  other than the pages your link takes you to, or share your link with anyone
  else. If you notice something that looks like a security problem, do not
  investigate it — just tell us what you saw.
- Your invitation link is personal to this test and expires after 30 days. We
  will disable it when the test is finished.

Thank you — your notes go directly into what we fix before launch.
